## Honest release classification

This is a **functional, source-checked product prototype**. It is not yet a broadly promoted production service because the organization has not supplied named reviewers, approved campaigns, a CMS/authentication decision, authoritative 58-county and ZIP crosswalk data, secure contact/form operations, or qualified Spanish review.

Those are explicit launch gates, not hidden defects.
