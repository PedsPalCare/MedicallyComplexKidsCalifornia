# California program crosswalk

Research review date: **2026-07-18**

Programs may overlap but are not interchangeable. Authorization by one program does not establish eligibility or responsibility under another.

| System | Role and family entry point | Problem route | Publication caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medi-Cal for Kids & Teens / EPSDT | Health coverage for eligible children; start by identifying managed care vs fee-for-service | Plan appeal or State Hearing route depends on coverage | Never promise hours, vendors, frequency, or approval |
| CCS / Whole Child Model | Specialty authorization, case management, and approved-provider roles; use county CCS entry | First distinguish classic CCS, CCS-only/FFS, and Whole Child Model plan responsibility | CCS is not merely insurance; do not hard-code financial criteria |
| HCBA / WPCS | Waiver care management for people meeting current criteria; application route may depend on ZIP | Distinguish quality grievance, care-coordination grievance, and adverse action | Capacity, waitlist, reserve categories, and agency assignments are dynamic |
| Regional Centers / Early Start / SDP | Developmental intake, eligibility, service coordination, IFSP/IPP, and optional SDP pathway | Early Start and Lanterman appeal processes differ | Medical complexity and Early Start participation do not automatically establish later eligibility |
| IHSS for children | County-assessed personal care and covered in-home tasks | Recipient Notice of Action uses State Hearing route; provider enrollment is distinct | Do not promise hours, protective supervision, paramedical services, or parent-provider status |
| School / IDEA / 504 | Educational access, FAPE, school health, related services, technology, and transportation | Local resolution, state complaint, mediation, or due process depends on issue | Do not promise a one-to-one nurse; home/hospital instruction is distinct from special education |
| Pediatric palliative care / hospice | Palliative care may accompany treatment; eligible Medicaid-enrolled children may have concurrent hospice/curative care | Applicable Medi-Cal or private-plan appeal route | Do not describe the former Partners for Children waiver as enrolling |
| Emergency power | Utility Medical Baseline and local/utility preparedness routes | Utility-specific escalation and local emergency management | Medical Baseline does not guarantee power, priority restoration, batteries, or evacuation |
| Life transition | Separate planning for education, SSA, decision support, CCS, health transfer, Medi-Cal, and Regional Center | Use the governing program’s notice; there is no single transition appeal | Avoid universal cutoff statements; verify each rule and date |
